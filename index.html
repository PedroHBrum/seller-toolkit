How to Read Your Etsy Fee Breakdown

Many sellers price their items based only on material costs and get surprised when their payout arrives. Here is what each line in your calculation means:

1. Etsy Charges Fees on Shipping Too

The 6.5% transaction fee and 3% + $0.25 payment processing fee apply to the total amount the buyer pays—including shipping. If you offer "Free Shipping," make sure your actual postage cost is baked into the Item Selling Price.

2. The Offsite Ads Surprise (12% or 15%)

When a buyer clicks an Etsy ad on Google, Facebook, or Pinterest and buys from your shop within 30 days, Etsy charges an extra 15% (or 12% if your shop made over $10,000 in the past 12 months), capped at $100 per order. Test your prices with Offsite Ads turned on in the calculator above to make sure a single ad click doesn't wipe out your profit.

3. Using the Minimum Item Price Box

If your current profit margin is below 25%–30%, it is difficult to run sales discounts or absorb occasional returns. Enter your ideal margin in the left panel to see the exact price tag required to protect your profit.
