Should You Opt Out of Etsy Offsite Ads?

Etsy advertises listings on Google, Facebook, Instagram, and Pinterest at no upfront cost. You only pay the 15% (or 12%) fee when a shopper clicks an ad and buys from your shop within 30 days—even if they buy a different item from your store.

When staying opted in makes sense: If your net profit margin after the 15% ad fee is still positive (for example, 10% to 20%+), Offsite Ads bring incremental sales you might not have captured organically.

When opting out (or raising prices) is necessary: If Scenario B above turns red or leaves less than $1–$2 per order, a few ad-attributed orders can erase the profit of your organic sales. Either raise your base price using our Etsy Fee & Profit Calculator or opt out in your Shop Manager settings (available for shops under $10,000 in 12-month sales).
