Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 27, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information is processed when you use Seller Toolkit.

1. Calculator Inputs Stay in Your Browser

All numbers entered into our calculators and simulators are processed locally in your browser via client-side scripts. We do not collect, log, or transmit your pricing, cost, or margin inputs to our servers.

2. Analytics and Technical Logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider and web analytics tools may process standard technical request data (such as IP address, browser type, device category, referrer URL, and pages visited) to maintain security, prevent abuse, and measure aggregate traffic.

3. Cookies and Future Advertising / Newsletter

If you voluntarily subscribe to a newsletter in the future, your email address will only be used for the updates you requested and you can unsubscribe at any time. Third-party services (such as analytics, advertising partners, or affiliate programs) may use cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy policies.

4. External Links

Our pages contain links to external sites, including official marketplace documentation and third-party tools. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.
