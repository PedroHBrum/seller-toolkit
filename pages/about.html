About Seller Toolkit

Calculators that tell sellers what to price, what to title and what to stop doing.

Seller Toolkit was built for Etsy and print-on-demand (POD) sellers who want clear numbers before launching listings, running discounts, or absorbing shipping and Offsite Ads costs.

Browser-First Privacy

All calculations run directly inside your web browser. Your item prices, production costs, shipping expenses, and target margins are never sent to or stored on our servers.

How Fee Data Is Maintained

Fee schedules are kept in a centralized configuration file and reviewed regularly against official documentation.
