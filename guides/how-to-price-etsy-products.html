How to Price Etsy Products So You Still Profit After Fees

Picking a price that "feels right" or simply copying a competitor's listing is one of the fastest ways to work for free. A sustainable Etsy price starts with your costs and target net margin—then works backward.

Find your minimum price automatically: Use the Target Net Profit Margin box inside our Etsy Fee & Profit Calculator to see the floor price your listing needs.

4 Steps to Price With a Safety Margin

  1. Add up all direct costs: Include unit cost (materials or POD base + fulfillment), packaging supplies, and any unrecovered shipping cost.
  2. Account for percentage fees on the full cart: Remember that transaction and payment processing fees apply to the total charged to the buyer, including shipping.
  3. Leave room for promos and ad attribution: Test stress scenarios in the Etsy Offsite Ads Simulator before locking your retail price.
  4. Check perceived value in the market: Compare how your product stands out in quality, design, or customization once you know your floor price.
