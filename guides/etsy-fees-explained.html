Etsy Fees Explained: What You Actually Keep From a Sale

Many new sellers look at an order total on Etsy and assume most of it is profit—until they see the payout deposit. Understanding how each fee stacks up is the fastest way to stop underpricing your products.

The Core Etsy Seller Fees at a Glance

Fee Type Typical Rate (US) What It Applies To
Listing Fee $0.20 Each listing published or renewed (and each additional quantity sold on multi-quantity listings)
Transaction Fee 6.5% Total order amount charged to the buyer, including shipping and gift wrap
Payment Processing 3% + $0.25 Total order amount processed through Etsy Payments (US baseline)
Currency Conversion 2.5% Orders where listing currency differs from your shop payment account currency
Offsite Ads 15% or 12% Attributed ad orders (capped at $100 per order), depending on shop revenue tier

Why Total Order Value Matters

A common mistake is calculating percentage fees only on the item price. Etsy applies percentage fees to the total amount paid by the buyer. If you sell a $25 item and charge $5 for shipping, percentage fees are calculated on the full $30 order—not just $25.

Don't Forget Your Product and Shipping Costs

Etsy fees are only half of the equation. To find your true net profit, you must subtract both Etsy fees and your out-of-pocket costs—materials, blanks, print-on-demand (POD) fulfillment, packaging, and the actual postage label you buy.
