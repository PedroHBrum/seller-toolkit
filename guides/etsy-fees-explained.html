Etsy Fees Explained: What You Actually Keep From a Sale

Many new sellers look at an order total on Etsy and assume most of it is profit—until they see the payout deposit. Understanding how each fee stacks up is the fastest way to stop underpricing your products.

Want to test your exact numbers? Plug your item price, shipping, and product cost into our Etsy Fee & Profit Calculator to see your real net profit and profit margin in seconds.

The Core Etsy Seller Fees at a Glance

Fee TypeTypical Rate (US)What It Applies To
Listing Fee$0.20Each listing published or renewed (plus renewals for extra quantity sold)
Transaction Fee6.5%Total order amount charged to the buyer, including shipping and gift wrap
Payment Processing3% + $0.25Total order amount processed through Etsy Payments (US baseline)
Currency Conversion2.5%Orders where listing currency differs from your shop payment currency
Offsite Ads15% or 12%Attributed ad orders (capped at $100 per order), depending on shop revenue tier

Why Total Order Value Matters

A common mistake is calculating percentage fees only on the item price. Etsy applies percentage fees to the total amount paid by the buyer. If you sell a $25 item and charge $5 for shipping, percentage fees apply to the full $30 order.

Don't Forget Your Product and Shipping Costs

To find your true net profit, subtract both Etsy fees and your direct costs (materials or POD fulfillment, packaging, and actual postage). Test ad-attributed orders with the Etsy Offsite Ads Simulator.
